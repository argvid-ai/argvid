# Gen0.5 reference lamp and gimbal

## Responsibility

Document the fixed reference hardware boundary, BOM, assembly assumptions, mechanical constraints, and validation evidence.

## Non-goals

Production certification, mobile robotics, unlicensed industrial design, or permission to use the Argvid trademark on derivatives.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs are reviewed requirements and verified parts; outputs are license-cleared design sources, BOM revisions, assembly notes, and tests.

## Dependencies

Depends on safety limits, firmware interfaces, and an approved hardware license.

## Invariants

Unverified dimensions and hardware checks are marked pending; substitute parts require review.

## Start condition

Publish source designs only after the hardware license and safety test plan are approved.

## Ownership and review

Hardware owner with safety, supply-chain, and license review.

## Verification

BOM validation, assembly review, mechanical checks, electrical checks, and hardware-in-the-loop evidence.

## Public use cases

Enable independent review and reproduction of an approved public reference device and its safety assumptions.

## License and data

Source and documentation default to Apache-2.0. Use synthetic or explicitly licensed public inputs with recorded provenance; publication rights require human review. Third-party assets, weights, datasets, and hardware design sources need separate license review. This documented boundary is not an activated implementation, released compatibility guarantee, or completed HIL result.
